# Ideation And Validation

Use this document when asking an AI assistant for article ideas, keyword
clusters, content calendars, or validation before drafting.

## Ideation Prompt

```text
Using the attached site context, generate article ideas for this blog.

Goal:
[traffic / affiliate revenue / lead generation / topical authority / product education]

Audience:
[describe audience]

Constraints:
- Must fit the site's core business.
- Must have clear search intent.
- Must be useful without being generic.
- Avoid topics already covered unless you propose a better angle.

Return a table with:
- topic
- primary keyword
- search intent
- article angle
- why it fits the business
- funnel stage
- difficulty estimate
- priority score from 1-10
- recommended article formula
- image concept
```

## Validation Prompt

```text
Validate these article ideas against the attached site context.

For each idea, score:
- business fit: 1-10
- audience fit: 1-10
- search intent clarity: 1-10
- originality: 1-10
- monetization or conversion value: 1-10
- risk of generic/thin content: low/medium/high

Then mark each idea as:
- keep
- revise
- reject

For revised ideas, provide the stronger topic, keyword, title angle, and why.
```

## Topic Scoring Rubric

Score each topic from 1 to 10.

Business fit:
- 1-3: barely connected to the site.
- 4-6: related, but weak commercial or authority value.
- 7-8: clearly supports the site's positioning.
- 9-10: directly builds authority, trust, or revenue.

Search intent:
- 1-3: unclear what the searcher wants.
- 4-6: intent exists but article scope is fuzzy.
- 7-8: clear informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational intent.
- 9-10: intent is clear and the article can satisfy it better than competitors.

Originality:
- 1-3: generic topic with no unique angle.
- 4-6: common topic with some useful framing.
- 7-8: specific, practical, or audience-tailored angle.
- 9-10: strong perspective, examples, frameworks, or first-hand usefulness.

## Content Calendar Prompt

```text
Build a 30-day content plan for this site.

Use the attached site context and these topic pillars:
[paste pillars]

Return:
- week
- article title
- primary keyword
- search intent
- article formula
- internal links to include
- image prompt summary
- reason for order
```

## Red Flags

Reject or revise an idea if:

- It does not connect to the site's business.
- It requires expertise the site cannot credibly claim.
- It depends on current data that will become outdated quickly.
- It is too broad for one article.
- It is only a rewritten version of a common article.
- It could create legal, medical, or financial risk without careful caveats.
- It has no clear next step for the reader.
